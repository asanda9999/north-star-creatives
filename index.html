About Us

Nine years
in the making.

Newly named, not new to this.

North Star Creatives is a forward-thinking creative agency built on nine years of hands-on industry experience, formally founded in 2026 and based in Johannesburg.

We operate at the intersection of strategy, storytelling and digital communication — helping brands build relevance, influence and measurable impact through digital marketing, influencer marketing and content creation.

We develop thoughtful marketing and content ecosystems that extend beyond visibility, ensuring every brand touchpoint is intentional, consistent and aligned to long-term growth.

Guided by the idea of the north star, we serve as a constant reference point for brands navigating complexity, change and growth.

Our Mission

A steady reference point.

Founder & Account Director

Yolanda Mkhize

Yolanda brings nine years of strategic and account leadership to North Star Creatives — directing content, social and influencer strategy across telecoms, fintech, FMCG, beauty and retail.

She has held senior leadership roles including Social Media Account Director at Ogilvy, Head of Content & Community at Numeral XII — where she scaled operations across more than 12 fintech brands — and Head of Social Media at THINK Creative Africa.

Her strength lies in translating business objectives into brand strategy — owning client relationships end-to-end, leading new business pitches, driving commercial growth and building campaign work that brings strategy to life.

A believer in staying sharp, not simply experienced, Yolanda holds a National Certificate in Advertising: Digital Marketing from Red & Yellow Creative School of Business, alongside HubSpot certifications.
